How to use it

  1. Open instagram.com and log in.
  2. Go to a profile, post or reel and click the orange button in the bottom-right corner (or this extension's toolbar icon).
  3. Click Save this post or Save all from @user. Keep the tab open while it runs.

Your storage password is only ever sent to Bunny. Only save content you own or have permission to keep, and remember a public Pull Zone lets anyone with a link open the files.
